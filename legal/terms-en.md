## 4. User account and personal data

Comxow can be used entirely locally, without needing to register. Creating an account is only necessary to save works to the cloud and publish them, and it only requires an email address; Comxow does not request or verify the real name, identity, or any other data of the person who owns that email address.

The email address is used solely to manage the account (sign-in, password recovery, and service-related notices), is kept for as long as the account remains active, and is deleted if the user requests to close it. It is stored using Supabase (with a data center in Frankfurt, European Union) and, when needed to send transactional communications, processed through Brevo (an EU-based email provider). Comxow does not sell or share this data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.

Other Comxow users can only see the chosen username, along with any other contact details the author chooses to voluntarily include in their published or shared works (for example, a link to their social media). Comxow does not collect, verify, or list this information voluntarily added by the author within their own work.

Comxow does not use tracking or advertising cookies, or third-party analytics tools. The application stores certain technical information on the user's own device (for example, the chosen language, whether these terms have already been accepted, or locally saved works) solely so the application works correctly and to help preserve the user's creations; this information is not transmitted to Comxow's servers unless the user themself chooses to save or publish a work to the cloud.

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their email address and account at any time, and exercise the other rights granted under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), by writing to contacto@comxow.com. Users also have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es) if they believe their data has not been processed in accordance with the law.

Beyond the account itself, to save works to the cloud and publish them the user is responsible for keeping their password confidential and for all activity carried out from their account.
